Water Sports Rental Sales Dashboard

1. Project Overview

I created this Microsoft Excel dashboard to analyse sales performance for a water-sports rental business. The goal was to identify sales patterns, compare team-member performance, understand customer behaviour, and discover opportunities to improve revenue.

2. Dashboard Features

- Key Metrics: Total revenue, number of rentals, average revenue per rental, and peak month.
- Sales Trends: Monthly and weekly performance.
- Customer Analysis: Revenue by customer type.
- Team Performance: Sales comparisons by team member.
- Top Rental Items: Five items generating the highest sales revenue.
- Interactive Filters: Explore performance by individual team member.

3. Key Insights

Seasonal Sales Patterns

July was the peak month for seven out of ten team members, while August was the peak for two and June for one. This suggests a possible seasonal pattern, although the overall business peak month needs to be confirmed using total monthly sales.

Business opportunity: Use historical sales patterns to prepare staff and equipment for busy periods and target quieter months with planned promotions.

Customer-Type Revenue

Membership revenue was relatively low in most team-member views, while group/event bookings were also among the lower-revenue categories. This raises questions about membership uptake, repeat bookings, and customer spending.

Business opportunity: Test limited membership benefits to encourage repeat rentals and attract bookings during quieter periods. Compare booking numbers, revenue, discounts, and repeat visits to determine whether the offer works.

Rental Item Opportunities

The Top Five Rental Items chart identifies products generating substantial revenue in the selected view.

Business opportunity: Test group packages featuring suitable popular items, such as kayaks and paddleboards, during quieter periods. Check rental demand, equipment costs, and availability before setting prices.

4. Recommended Strategy

Run a small off-peak promotion pilot using membership benefits or group rental packages. Compare results against similar periods without the promotion.

Measure rental numbers, revenue, average revenue per rental, discount costs, and estimated profit contribution. More bookings alone do not guarantee greater profit, so the business should assess the financial results before expanding the campaign.

5. Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Charts, summary metrics, and interactive filters
- Data interpretation and business recommendations

6. Project Outcome and Limitations

This project demonstrates how sales data can be transformed into a dashboard that supports business decisions. The analysis identifies patterns worth investigating and proposes measurable strategies for improving sales during quieter periods.

The recommendations require further testing against actual business results. Dashboard screenshots are included in this repository; the interactive Excel workbook is not hosted here unless uploaded separately.
